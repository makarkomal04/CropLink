# User Wallet Guide - CropLink

## What is a Crypto Wallet?

A cryptocurrency wallet allows you to:
- Receive payments in MATIC (Polygon network cryptocurrency)
- Send payments to others
- Store your digital assets securely
- Make purchases on the CropLink platform

## Creating Your Wallet

### Web (Browser)

1. Login to your CropLink account
2. Click "Wallet" in the navigation menu
3. Click "Create Wallet" button
4. **IMPORTANT**: Save your 12-word recovery phrase!
   - Write it down on paper
   - Store it in a safe place
   - Never share it with anyone
   - You'll need it to recover your wallet if you lose access

5. Confirm you've saved the recovery phrase
6. Your wallet is now created!

### Android App

1. Open CropLink app and login
2. Tap the menu icon (☰)
3. Select "Wallet"
4. Tap "Create Wallet"
5. **Write down your recovery phrase** (12 words)
6. Confirm the recovery phrase
7. Wallet created successfully!

## Understanding Your Wallet

### Wallet Address
- Example: `0x742d35Cc6634C0532925a3b844Bc9e7595f0bEb1`
- Think of it like your bank account number
- Safe to share with others to receive payments
- Used for all transactions

### Balance
- Shown in MATIC tokens
- 1 MATIC can be used for multiple transactions
- Transaction fees are very low (~$0.01)

### Recovery Phrase (Seed Phrase)
- 12 random words
- **The master key to your wallet**
- Anyone with this phrase can access your funds
- No one, including CropLink, can recover your funds without it

## Receiving Payments

1. Open your wallet
2. Click/tap "Receive"
3. Share your wallet address or QR code
4. Wait for the transaction to confirm (~30 seconds)
5. Balance will update automatically

## Making Payments

### Buying Products

1. Add items to cart
2. Go to checkout
3. Select "Pay with Crypto Wallet"
4. Review the payment details:
   - Product total
   - Platform fee (2%)
   - Gas fee (~$0.01)
5. Click "Confirm Payment"
6. Wait for confirmation
7. Your order is placed!

### Sending to Another User

1. Go to Walletpage
2. Click "Send"
3. Enter recipient's wallet address or scan QR code
4. Enter amount in MATIC
5. Review and confirm
6. Transaction sent!

## Security Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
- **Save your recovery phrase** on paper in a safe place
- Keep your recovery phrase private
- Make a backup copy and store it separately
- Use a strong password for your account
- Log out when using shared devices

### ❌ DON'T:
- Share your recovery phrase with anyone
- Store recovery phrase digitally (screenshots, email, cloud)
- Click suspicious links claiming to be from CropLink
- Share your private key (different from wallet address)
- Ignore security warnings

## Transaction Timeline

1. **Initiated**: Payment sent to blockchain
2. **Pending**: Waiting for network confirmation (~10-30 seconds)
3. **Confirmed**: Transaction recorded on blockchain
4. **Completed**: Funds transferred successfully

## Understanding Escrow

When you make a purchase:
1. Your payment is held in **escrow** (smart contract)
2. Farmer ships the product
3. You confirm delivery
4. Payment is automatically released to farmer
5. Platform fee (2%) goes to CropLink

This protects both buyers and sellers!

## Viewing Transaction History

### Web
1. Go to Wallet page

3. View all incoming and outgoing transactions
4. Click any transaction to see details on Polygonscan

### Android

## Wallet Recovery

If you lose access to your wallet:

1. Click "Import Wallet" or "Restore Wallet"
2. Enter your 12-word recovery phrase
3. Wallet will be restored with full balance
4. All transaction history remains on blockchain

**⚠️ Without your recovery phrase, funds cannot be recovered!**

## Transaction Fees Explained

- **Platform Fee**: 2% of order value (goes to CropLink)
- **Gas Fee**: ~$0.01-0.05 (goes to network validators)
- **Total**: Your payment + 2% + gas fee

Example:
- Product: 10 MATIC
- Platform fee: 0.2 MATIC (2%)
- Gas fee: 0.01 MATIC
- **Total**: 10.21 MATIC

## Troubleshooting

### "Insufficient balance" error
- Check your wallet balance
- Make sure you have enough for payment + gas fees
- Add more MATIC to your wallet

### Transaction stuck as "Pending"
- Wait 1-2 minutes, network may be busy
- Check status on Polygonscan
- Contact support if stuck over 10 minutes

### Lost recovery phrase
- Unfortunately, funds cannot be recovered
- This is why saving the phrase is critical
- Create a new wallet and start fresh

### Wrong amount sent
- Blockchain transactions are irreversible
- Double-check amounts before confirming
- Contact recipient for potential refund
